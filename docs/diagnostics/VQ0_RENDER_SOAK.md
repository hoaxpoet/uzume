# VQ.0 render soak (2026-10-22)

**Build:** Release, commit `ee218841` (`vq-0`). **Machine:** Apple M2 Pro Mac mini, 16 GPU cores, 16 GB. **How:**
`Scripts/render_soak.sh … --override 3840x2160 --toggle 300 --minutes 60` — the app's real scene path, every certified
scene this Mac plans (28) for 20 s each (one roster cycle ≈ 9.3 min), the drawable forced to 3840×2160 with the BR.6b
cap in force (tier 1: scenes render at a 2560×1440 canvas) and toggled to 1920×1080 for 5 minutes every 5 minutes;
track *Dance Yrself Clean* from the stem cache, looping. Footprint sampled by the script every 2 s; the app writes one
row per minute. Switch frames = the 1.5 s after a scene switch or a committed resize, reported apart.

Both soaks ran with the 532 MiB ceiling the certified scenes alone give. The final ceiling is 858 MiB (it also
covers a reconstructing scene, D-264), so the soaks tested the stricter budget.
