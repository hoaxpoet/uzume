# Fiddlehead — visual references

Matt's direction supersedes these images as a fidelity target (2026-10-06/07). In his words:

- "First, it must be recognizable as a fern in shape and texture. Then, it must have the fractal pattern at different levels of zoom — a staggering amount of detail."
- "The camera is on a continuous zoom into the details of the fern, which reveals the same pattern repeating and unfolding."
- "The fern should be uncurling as the camera zooms in."
- "The music can create shimmers or rippling waves down the body of the fern, like a nerve impulse traveling along trunks and branches."
- "It should be a vibrant light show. A bath of color and mesmerizing patterns."
- "It doesn't necessarily need to be a 'psychedelic' palette, but it should be awesome whatever palette you choose."

## Mandatory traits

1. Reads as a fern (stem, paired leaflets, curled tips) at EVERY zoom level.
2. The same pattern repeats at every scale; the camera never runs out of detail.
3. Fronds unfurl as the camera reaches them.
4. A colour bath: emissive, saturated, light on edges and veins; never black (D-037), never washed to white.
5. Beat impulses travel trunk → branches → leaflet tips.

## Anti-references

- A flat lace / doily pattern (FH.13–14's lit renders).
- A single framed 3-D object (the reference's composition).
- Pastel or grey colour (sub-pixel hue mixing, FH.15 colour pass).
- Hard cut-offs on frond tips (FH.15).

## Musical role

The dive is the bed; **every beat on the cached grid is a nerve impulse that leaves the trunk and fans out to every leaflet tip**, downbeats brighter, while bass deviation swells the light riding the colour bands and the passage's loudness sets the exposure.

## Temporal contract

- Continuous: one zoom level per 7 s, seamless; colour bands flow outward along the branches.
- Per beat (grid): an impulse front crosses one stem in ~2 s.
- Per bar: downbeat impulses at 0.7 strength vs 0.3.
- Section scale: exposure follows `spectralSurge`.
- Silence: the dive keeps falling and the colour keeps flowing at low exposure; no impulses (no beats). It coasts, it does not freeze — Matt's call to confirm at M7.
