# Preset roster review — Matt's observations (2026-09-04)

**Source of truth for Phase PR.** Matt watched the full roster against David Bowie — *Low*
(local FLAC, 11 tracks, the local-file path) and wrote these per-preset observations.

**Recorded verbatim 2026-09-09.** Until then the notes existed only in chat: `ENGINEERING_PLAN.md`
said *"wrote 25 per-preset observations"* and then carried a **synthesis** of them — a three-row
cause table — plus four quoted fragments scattered across three sections. With no artifact to check
against, PR.2 was built on that synthesis and could not be verified against the source. Matt,
2026-09-09: *"I'm not convinced that PR.2 is necessary; I worry that it is a misinterpretation of my
notes."* He was right, and the absence of this file is why nobody could tell.

**Do not paraphrase these.** Quote them. Where an increment claims to address one, it cites the
preset heading here.

---

## Witchlight
Inconsistent with downbeat.
Want more looping
Why is this first in the sequence vs. last?

## Aurora Veil
Looks great. Stars blink well with the beat.
Wish there was more color variation, purple is fleeting.

## Cymatic Resonance
One of the best to watch. Wish there were more presets like this one.
I want to see more variation of the patterns it creates.

## Cytokinesis (Radishes)
Remove overlap between dividing cells.
Hangs for seconds before restart

## Dragon Bloom
Washed out, extreme brightness. Reds look gorgeous. Would like to see the same saturated color across the visible light spectrum.

## Fata Morgana
Too dark, want more reflection of the pulsars in the water. Recommend reducing the size of the water relative to the sky - sky should take up a larger percentage of the canvas. Then pulsars can be larger.
Not sure how the preset is programmed to sync with the music, but it's not apparent.

## Ferrofluid Ocean
Brilliant sync with the music, but everything seems like 4/4 time.

## Filigree (Cheetah)
Not sure how the preset is programmed to sync with the music, seems like it's a movie on a loop
Speed of music could be better tied to speed of the motion? Perhaps.

## Floret
Pulses to the beat but more connection to the music could be obvious.
Mesmerizing but also kinda boring

## Fractal Tree
Fun, it just dances along with the music pretty convincingly
Might consider adding additional trees so dancing is synchronized

## Glaze
Jumps from top to bottom of screen too frequently - I'd like to see more movement within the bounds of the canvas / screen.
I'd like to see more mixture and blending of colors. Also quite bright

## Gossamer
Tuning for sync with music, has potential.

## Lumen Mosaic
Sync with music could probably be improved. Downbeat and beat.
Nice mood variation across tracks

## Membrane
Sync with music is weak, puddle pulse could be improved visually and with respect to motion.

## Meniscus
Sync with music is weak, puddle ripple looks good but timing could be improved

## Cell Division (Mitosis)
Sync with music is tenuous. Speed is seemingly uniform

## Murmuration
Beautiful. Cloud could be bigger, take up more of the screen

## Nacre
Slow down the speed slightly or make it match the tempo of the music. Too fast overall.

## Nebula
Needs better sync with music. Spikes are too sporadic. Should look more activated.

## Nimbus
Doesn't do much and yet people seem to really like it and think the ball has a personality

## Plasma
Needs better sync with the music, very jittery for Bowie's Low.

## Ricercar
Need to test with more tracks. Not clear or strong music sync with Bowie's Subterraneans

## Skein
Fine enough

## Spectral Cartograph
Opportunities to clean up eventually

## Staged Sandbox
Get rid of it.

## Stave
A little hazy. Maybe make a little crisper? Not sure.

## Volumetric Lithograph
Not strong sync with music
